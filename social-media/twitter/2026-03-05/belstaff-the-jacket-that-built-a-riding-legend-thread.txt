Che Guevara wore one while riding across South America in 1952. Steve McQueen wore one while racing motorcycles in Scotland. A woman named Mary Kenyon-Slaney wore one riding through the Scottish Highlands in 1948. The jacket was always a Belstaff.

--- Tweet 4/6 ---
Belstaff didn't invent the motorcycle jacket. But it made the best one — a jacket that could protect you from rain, wind, gravel, and cold, while still looking great when you got off the bike.
