In 1837, a young German craftsman named Thierry Hermès opened a small workshop in Paris. He didn't make handbags or scarves. He made harnesses and bridles for horses. His work was so good that riders came from all over Europe to buy from him.

--- Tweet 4/6 ---
Today, his family's company is worth over $200 billion. People wait years to buy its most famous handbag. But walk into any Hermès store and you'll notice something special:the horse is still everywhere. In the logo. In the leather. In the soul of the brand.

--- Tweet 5/6 ---
Thierry Hermès built his name making the finest horse gear in Paris. His harnesses won prizes at world exhibitions in 1855 and 1867. Royals and wealthy horse owners became his loyal customers.
